Prompt Writing Academy

A good prompt gives the AI a clear task, audience, format and level of detail.

Weak prompt

Tell me about volcanoes.

This is too broad and gives very little guidance.

Better prompt

Explain volcanoes to a Year 8 student using simple language.

This identifies the audience and writing style.

Strong prompt

Explain volcanoes to a Year 8 student using simple language, one real-world example and five quiz questions.

This clearly describes the required output.

AI Safety

Protect personal data

Never share passwords, home addresses, private documents or sensitive information.

Check important facts

Compare AI answers with textbooks, teachers and trusted websites.

Use your own thinking

AI should support your learning rather than complete every task for you.

Follow school rules

Use AI only in ways allowed by your teacher and school policy.

Respect copyright

Do not copy protected text, images or music without permission.

Be transparent

Acknowledge AI support when your teacher or task requires it.

AI Ethics

Bias

AI can reflect unfair patterns found in its training data. Ask whether an answer treats people fairly.

Copyright

AI-generated content may be influenced by existing work. Always respect original creators.

Human responsibility

People remain responsible for important decisions. AI should not replace human judgement.
